The problem

Your build budget is leaking. Mostly into mark-ups you never see.

The kitchen, the windows and the doors are often made in the same factories overseas. By the time they reach you, a distributor, a wholesaler and a showroom have each taken a cut.

Going direct used to mean guessing which supplier is real, chasing samples across time zones and hoping the order turns up right. OwnerSourced takes that risk off the table.
